Privacy Policy – Kallan & Police

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Kallan & Police is an offline party game played by passing one phone around. It does not collect, upload, sell or share any personal data.

What stays on your phone

The app saves only two things, and only on your device:

  • Your chosen language.
  • The points you set for each role, if you change them.

Player names you type are kept in memory only for the current game and are never saved or uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes everything.

Internet

The game works fully offline and does not connect to the internet.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo access.
  • No sale or sharing of data with third parties.

Children

The game is meant for teens and adults playing together, and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions? Use the developer contact email shown on the app's Google Play listing.
